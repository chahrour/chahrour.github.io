Before the exam

  1. Stow devices; bags along the walls at the ends of the rows.
  2. Collect your exam from front of room. (Cornell ID, if you have it).
  3. Do not open the exam.
  4. Leave at least one empty seat between you and others. We may move you.
  5. Write your name at the top.
  6. Instructor will announce start of work.
  7. After the exam begins, add your NetID to each page (bottom-right).

End of exam

  • Official time: screen at the front. Warnings at 15, 5, and 1 min.
  • Finished?
    • 10+ min left: quietly hand in and leave.
    • Under 10 min left: stay until time is called.
  • Stop immediately when time is called.
  • Pass your exam to the aisle. Stay seated until it is collected.
  • Keep or discard your scratch page and notes sheet.
  • No devices until you are outside the room.

Restroom

  • Sign out/in with the TA by the door. You may be asked to wait.
  • Do not leave the room with a device in your possession, or your exam will not be accepted.
